01 / Introduction

Our Brand

Layot Fab Styles is a premium fashion house dedicated to empowering elegance, celebrating femininity, and crafting timeless pieces that inspire confidence.

Every detail of our brand identity reflects our passion for sophistication, quality, and the art of style. This document serves as the foundation for our visual and verbal identity.

Brand Story

The Journey of Elegance

The Beginning

Founded with a vision to redefine modern elegance through meticulous craftsmanship and bespoke designs.

Growth

Our collections grew, reaching more individuals who love timeless style, expanding into ready-to-wear and bridal.

The Future

We continue to innovate and inspire with fashion that stands the test of time, maintaining our luxurious roots.

Photography

Visual Tone

Imagery should evoke feelings of premium quality, warmth, and timeless elegance. Focus on minimalist backgrounds, natural warm lighting, and confident, poised subjects.

Brand Voice

How We Speak
Tone of Voice
  • Elegant & Sophisticated
  • Warm & Inviting
  • Timeless & Confident
  • Professional yet Approachable
What to Avoid
  • Trendy internet slang
  • Aggressive sales language
  • Overuse of emojis
  • Cheap marketing gimmicks

Pattern System

Our subtle repeating pattern is crafted from our monogram. It features extremely low contrast, providing a woven textile inspiration perfect for shopping bags, tissue paper and digital backgrounds without overpowering the main content.
