Our Heritage

The Story

Layot Fab Styles began with a simple yet profound belief—every woman deserves to feel beautiful, confident, and truly seen in what she wears.

From designing bespoke pieces for close friends to cultivating a growing family of clients worldwide, our journey has been shaped by passion, hard work, and an enduring love for timeless fashion. We never sought to follow fleeting trends, but rather to establish a standard of enduring elegance.

We are deeply committed to exceptional craftsmanship. Every cut, every stitch, and every silhouette is meticulously planned to enhance the individual's natural grace. Fashion, to us, is the ultimate expression of personal confidence.

Today, we stand as a brand rooted in elegance, defined by craftsmanship, and guided by our commitment to making every client feel exceptional.

Meet The Founder

Akindiyo Titilayo

Founder & Creative Director

I have always viewed fabric as a canvas—a medium through which I can translate my vision of elegance into reality. My inspiration stems from the strength, grace, and multifaceted nature of the women I design for.

My philosophy is simple: clothing should never overpower the wearer, but rather elevate her presence. I want to build a future where Layot Fab Styles continues to be a sanctuary of bespoke luxury, a place where artistry meets individual expression.

Our Process

3. Fabric Selection

4. Measurements

Accurate measurements are taken to achieve the perfect bespoke fit.

5. Tailoring

6. Final Delivery

After final fitting and quality checks, your masterpiece is ready.
